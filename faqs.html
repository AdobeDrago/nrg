Most frequently asked

What is a fixed-rate energy plan?

With a fixed-rate energy plan, the price you pay for electricity each billing period will not change for the duration of your contract. However, the price that your TDSP charges to deliver electricity to your home may change if the Public Utility Commission approves the change. If that happens, the TDSP charges on your bill would increase, but all other charges (energy charge, base charge or usage charge) remain fixed.

What is a variable-rate energy plan?

Variable-rate energy plans offer flexibility for customers who aren't ready for a set contract. Customers on a variable-rate plan can cancel anytime without penalty. However, the rate you pay for electricity may vary from month to month.

Will I need to pay a deposit?

Everything Energy doesn't charge deposits, but once you select a plan and provider, we'll send you to their website to complete your enrollment. Retail Electricity Providers may require customers to pay a deposit. During enrollment, you'll learn of any deposit requirements.

What happens if I move?

If you're moving to a new address, your electricity provider may be able to transfer your service to your new location. Many suppliers offer convenient options to request a transfer online. If you choose to switch providers during your move, you can generally do so without being charged a cancellation fee.

Do I need to pay to use Everything Energy?

No. Everything Energy's website is completely free to use. You can explore, compare, and shop for electricity plans at no cost. Everything Energy receives a commission from partner REPs when a shopper completes an enrollment through our site.

Is Everything Energy affiliated with any electricity suppliers?

Everything Energy is affiliated with the following retail electricity providers in Texas: Reliant Energy, Green Mountain Energy, Direct Energy, Cirro Energy, and Discount Power — all subsidiaries of or affiliated with NRG Energy, Inc. In Pennsylvania, we are affiliated with Green Mountain Energy Company, Direct Energy Services LLC, and Reliant Energy Northeast LLC (dba NRG Home).
