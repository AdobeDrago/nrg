About Everything Energy

Better shopping comparisons for smart electricity shoppers.

Everything Energy is a comparison site for smart electricity shoppers in Texas and Pennsylvania. Our goal is to make shopping easy so that consumers can pick the right electricity plan for their family and lifestyle. You'll see us rolling out helpful shopping tools regularly, including tools that help you shop based on your actual usage and feedback from other Everything Energy shoppers on providers and plans.

Everything Energy is part of the NRG family of companies, where a customer-focused mentality has led to the creation of technologies and tools that empower residents and organizations to think critically about their electricity, including where their power is coming from, what impact it has on the environment and how they can become more conscious energy users. NRG is committed to delivering cleaner energy and delivering customer-focused solutions.

Our partner providers

Everything Energy partners with NRG, Reliant Energy, Green Mountain Energy, Direct Energy, Cirro Energy, and Discount Power. These are stable, reliable providers with strong customer service records that have been in the Texas and Pennsylvania markets for decades, so you know you can count on them to serve you well now, and into the future.
