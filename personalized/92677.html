Pool owner savings tips

Maximize your electricity plan by optimizing how and when you run your pool equipment.

Shift pool pump to off-peak hours

Run your pool pump from 8pm to 6am on the Truly Free Nights plan and pay zero for pool electricity. A variable-speed pump uses 1,500–2,500 kWh/year — shifting to free nights saves $150–$400 annually.

Use a variable-speed pump

Variable-speed pool pumps use up to 80% less energy than single-speed pumps. Run on low speed for longer periods to save even more. Pair with a flat-rate plan like Smart Simple for predictable billing.

Save up to $450/year

Pool heater timing

Gas pool heaters are the #1 energy drain for pool owners. Use a solar cover to retain heat overnight and only run the heater 1–2 hours before use. On the Truly Free Weekends plan, pre-heat on Saturday mornings for free.

Save up to $200/year

LED pool lighting

Replace incandescent pool lights with LED equivalents. LED pool lights use 75% less energy and last 10x longer. On any fixed-rate plan, this reduces your baseline usage and keeps bills predictable.
